1. FTI-SCF Integration in 22 remaining SBI Foreign Offices by within 31st December 2026;

Mode of Procurement	Nomination		
Activity proposed	Application Development, Maintenance and Testing; Application Service Providers (ASPs) including ATM Switch ASPs;		
Background of the project	"Finastra Trade Innovation (FTI) is a Trade Finance & Supply Chain Finance (SCF) software from Finastra.FTI is a global trade finance engine which is being used by our Foreign Offices to process Letters of Credit,Guarantees,Collections and SCF. The integration of FTI & SCF has already been deployed for USA (Phase-I) and now the objective is integration of FTI with SCF product across the remaining 22 Foreign Offices (Phase-2). The key deliverables under phase-2 are as follows:
a) API Integration : Integrate with Credible (a supply chain finance platform) through enterprise service bus (ESB),
b) FTI Customization by adding new field layouts alongwith integration of extra data fields in API,
c) Front-end Configuration in FTI through new SCF product setupworkflow orchestrations,Interest & Charges product-type wise mapping,security in user roles and groups and External system definition,
d) Reporting : Customized report development for SCF product.
In this regard,a proposal along with the details of commercials,terms & conditions and timeline has been submitted by Wipro Ltd. (attached as Annexure-I) , who is providing professional & technical services for customization,support and maintanance of the application since inception.  

2. In addition to the above,there is a need to upgrade FTI and Finastra Corporate Channel (FCC) to latest supported versions for all the 23 foreign offices.The expected outcome from this upgradation will be long-term stability,regulatory readiness for SWIFT SR2026,and FTI-FCC compatibility across all foreign offices.The key changes in FTI & FCC will be : 
a) Discontinuation of unstructured addresss from Nov 2026,
b) There will be new fields in HS Code & Incoterms in trade messages,
c) Revisions to party fileds in MT4xx,MT7xx,MT798,and
d) NACKs prevention through enhanced network validations.
In this regard,a proposal along with the details of commercials,terms & conditions and timeline has been submitted by Wipro Ltd. (attached as Annexure-II) , who is providing professional & technical services for customization,support and maintanance of the application since inception.  

3. Another important requirement needs to be taken into consideration for development and enhancements as per regulatory changes in Bangladesh,focused on import letter of credit (ILC) module,customer and bill of entry.The key deliverables under this will be : 
a) Customized new fields in ILC module for pre-shipment inspection,
b) Customer master updation,
c) New customer fields in UI,
d) New Module for Bill of Entry detailswith back-end and front-end validations,and
e) Incoporation of of fetch logic for all new customized fields.
In this regard,a proposal along with the details of commercials,terms & conditions and timeline has been submitted by Wipro Ltd. (attached as Annexure-III) , who is providing professional & technical services for customization,support and maintanance of the application since inception.  
 
4. The total proposed outlay to deliver these development,integration and enhancements will be Rs. 3,82,22,000.00 (Rupess Three Crore Eighty Two Lakh & Twenty Two Thousand only)."

2.  Digital investment journey for NRI customers will enable transaction capabilities thereby supporting business growth in the NRI segment.
